About Us

Learning that feels like play

Qwerty Academy is a free learning website for kids aged 6 to 16. Short lessons, friendly quizzes, and a robot teacher who never runs out of patience.

Why we built it

We think practising school subjects should feel like a game, not a chore. Every lesson on Qwerty Academy is small enough to finish in a few minutes, checks your answer straight away, and explains the right answer in plain, friendly language so kids learn from mistakes instead of feeling bad about them.

Meet Robo

Robo is our robot teacher. He introduces every subject, cheers when you get an answer right, and gives a little tip when you don't. He never gets tired, never gets grumpy, and always has time for one more question.
