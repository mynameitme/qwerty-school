Meet your teacher

Robo is a friendly robot who loves to explain things. He never gets tired, never gets grumpy, and always has time for one more question.

He points at the board, shows you step by step, and cheers when you get it right!

How a lesson works

Every lesson is short and fun. First Robo shows an example, then you try it yourself, and then you both check the answer together.
